This survey is open to people who work with trees. Please use the link from your invitation.

Does this tree look right?

You will see leaf-off trees drawn as branch skeletons, with a person for scale. Some come from laser scans of real trees, some from a computer growth model. Each one is labelled with its species and grew in a forest stand.

Rate each tree from 1 (clearly wrong) to 5 (looks completely right) for that species. After each answer we tell you where the tree came from. Rate as many as you like; you can stop at any time.

Fine twigs are left out for every tree, scanned and generated alike: please judge the stem and branch architecture.
