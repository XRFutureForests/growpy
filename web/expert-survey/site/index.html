Tree Architecture Survey

XR Future Forests Lab · University of Freiburg

Which tree is this, and does it look like one?

We grow trees with a procedural model for a VR forest twin and want to know how close they come to real trees. You will see about 40 leaf-off trees, drawn as plain branch skeletons. Some are generated, some are drawn from laser scans of real trees. All are drawn the same way.

For each tree:

  • name the species you think it is (or say you can't tell);
  • say whether you think it is real or computer-generated, and how natural the branching looks;
  • after that, we tell you which species it is meant to be. You rate how typical the tree is for that species and can mark what looks off.

About 20–25 minutes. You can stop at any time; your answers stay in this browser and you can resume later. Fine twigs are not drawn, for real and generated trees alike: please judge the stem and branch architecture, not twig density.
